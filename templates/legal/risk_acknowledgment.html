Medical and Travel Risk Acknowledgment

Operated by: Thipak’s LLC, North Carolina  |  Effective date: 11/4/2026

Before booking or paying for medical travel, every patient must acknowledge and accept that:

  • • Procedure Risks: All medical, surgical, dental, and cosmetic procedures carry risks, including pain, infection, bleeding, scarring, blood clots, anesthesia reactions, unsatisfactory results, need for further procedures, long-term complications, and in rare cases death.
  • • Travel Risks: Travelling for treatment adds risks: long flights and reduced mobility after surgery can increase the risk of blood clots (DVT/PE) and other complications; I may need to extend my stay unexpectedly; and medical standards, regulation, drug names, and patient rights vary by country.
  • • Home Healthcare & Insurer Coverage: If complications occur after I return home, my local healthcare system may not be obliged to treat me for free, and my insurer may not cover me.
  • • Legal Remedies Abroad: Legal remedies, including malpractice claims, may be limited, slow, or unavailable in the provider's country, and I may have to bring any claim in that country.
  • • Post-Operative Instructions: I will follow the provider's pre- and post-operative instructions, will not travel before the provider says it is safe to do so, and will tell my local doctor about my treatment.
  • • Facilitator Role: TravelNTreat.com is a facilitator and is not responsible for the medical care I receive.

I have had the chance to ask the provider questions and to consult my own doctor. I accept these risks voluntarily.
