Health Consent

Consent to Process Health Information & Cross-Border Transfer

Operated by: Thipak’s LLC, North Carolina  |  Effective date: 11/4/2026

1. Explicit Consent to Process Health Information

I, the user, give my explicit consent to Thipak’s LLC and TravelNTreat.com to collect, store, and process the health-related information I provide, including medical history, diagnoses, medications, allergies, images, test results, and treatment interests, for these purposes only:

  • (a) assessing my request and matching me with suitable providers;
  • (b) sending this information to the providers I choose or am matched with so they can evaluate my case and provide quotes and treatment plans;
  • (c) coordinating my booking and travel; and
  • (d) providing customer support and resolving complaints.

I understand that: the providers who receive my information are independent organizations that will handle it under their own policies and local laws; my information may be transferred outside my country (see Section 2 below); TravelNTreat.com does not use my health information for advertising and does not sell it; I can withdraw my consent at any time by writing to info@thipaksllc.com and I can request access to, correction of, or deletion of my information; and withdrawal does not affect processing already done and may mean TravelNTreat.com cannot continue to arrange my booking. I give this consent freely and it is not a condition of using the general parts of the website.

2. Cross-Border Data Transfer Consent

I understand that my personal and health information will be shared with TravelNTreat.com’s service providers and with the healthcare providers I select, who may be located in any country in the world. These countries may not provide the same level of data protection as my home country, and I may have fewer rights or less effective remedies there. TravelNTreat.com uses contractual safeguards where required, but cannot control a provider's own handling of data once received. I consent to these transfers for the purposes stated in the Health Data Consent.
