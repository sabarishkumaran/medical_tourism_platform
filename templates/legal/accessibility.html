TravelNTreat.com Accessibility Statement

Operated by: Thipak’s LLC, North Carolina  |  Effective date: 11/4/2026

Thipak’s LLC is committed to making TravelNTreat.com usable by everyone, including people with disabilities. We aim to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA.

We test regularly and fix issues as we find them. Some content provided by third parties may not yet be fully accessible.

Need Assistance or Alternative Formats?

If you have difficulty using any part of the site or need information in an alternative format, contact us at info@thipaksllc.com. We will reply within 7 business days and work to provide the information or service you need.
