Community Guidelines

Acceptable Use, Community Guidelines and Review Policy

Operated by: Thipak’s LLC, North Carolina  |  Effective date: 11/4/2026

  1. Authentic Experience: Reviews and posts must reflect your own genuine experience, be truthful, and be respectful. No threats, hate speech, harassment, or discrimination.
  2. Privacy Protection: Do not post other people's private information or medical records, including identifying details of other patients or staff, without explicit consent.
  3. No Incentivized or Conflicted Reviews: Do not post reviews in exchange for payment or other benefits unless clearly disclosed, and do not post reviews of your own business or a competitor's.
  4. No Commercial Spam: Do not post spam, advertisements, or links to unrelated services.
  5. No Unlawful Medical Requests: Do not request or offer prescription medicines, controlled substances, or services that are illegal where they would be provided.
  6. Moderation & Enforcement: We may edit, decline, or remove content and suspend accounts that break these rules. Providers may respond to reviews but may not pressure users to remove them.
  7. Reporting Abuse: Report abuse, violations, or illicit activity to info@thipaksllc.com.
