# MedTour: Business Model & Strategic Document

## 1. Executive Summary
MedTour is a digital marketplace for the medical tourism industry. It addresses the global disparity in healthcare costs by connecting patients in high-cost regions (e.g., USA, UK) with high-quality, accredited healthcare providers in affordable regions (e.g., India, Turkey, Mexico). The platform monetizes through a hybrid model of hospital subscriptions and lead-generation commissions.

---

## 2. Market Problem & Opportunity
### The Problem
*   **Cost Barrier**: Quality medical procedures (dental, orthopedic, cardiac) are prohibitively expensive in developed nations.
*   **Information Gap**: Patients struggle to verify the quality and accreditation of international hospitals.
*   **Logistical Complexity**: Coordination between international patients and local hospitals is often manual and fragmented.

### The Opportunity
The medical tourism market is valued at billions of dollars and is growing as healthcare costs rise. MedTour captures this value by providing a centralized, trusted, and efficient booking platform.

---

## 3. Value Proposition

### For Patients
*   **Trust & Transparency**: Access to only accredited (JCI/NABH) facilities.
*   **Financial Savings**: Procedure costs up to 60-80% lower than domestic options.
*   **Concierge Experience**: Secure quote requests, doctor profiles, and aftercare tracking.

### For Hospitals
*   **Global Reach**: Instant access to an international patient base without massive marketing spend.
*   **Qualified Leads**: Inquiries are structured and intent-verified before reaching the hospital.
*   **Digital Management**: Tools to manage treatment packages, doctors, and patient communications in one dashboard.

---

## 4. Revenue Model

MedTour utilizes a three-tiered revenue strategy to ensure steady cash flow and scalability:

### A. Hospital Subscription Fees (SaaS Revenue)
Hospitals pay a monthly fee for platform access and visibility:
*   **Basic ($0)**: Entry-level for small clinics.
*   **Premium ($199/mo)**: Standard tier for established hospitals; includes dedicated coordinator and higher search ranking.
*   **Elite Executive ($499/mo)**: Maximum exposure with homepage priority and unlimited service listings.

### B. Lead Generation & Commission (Transaction Revenue)
*   **Success Fees**: A percentage commission (e.g., 5-10%) charged to the hospital for every patient procedure confirmed through the platform.
*   **Elite Perk**: Elite hospitals receive a waiver on commissions for their first 25 leads each month to incentivize high-tier subscriptions.

### C. Ancierge & Service Fees (Future Revenue)
*   Optional service fees charged to patients for premium travel insurance, visa assistance, and 24/7 on-ground concierge support.

---

## 5. Operations & Scaling

### Quality Control
All participating hospitals must pass a manual verification process, providing digital copies of their medical certifications (e.g., JCI certificates), which are stored securely in the platform.

### Financial Integrity
The **Lead Wallet** system ensures that hospitals maintain a pre-paid balance for lead fees, reducing the risk of non-payment and simplifying international financial transactions.

### Expansion Strategy
1.  **Phase 1**: Focus on high-demand procedures (Dental, Cosmetic, Orthopedic).
2.  **Phase 2**: Expand to complex surgeries (Cardiac, Oncology).
3.  **Phase 3**: Partner with international insurance providers to offer "MedTour-ready" policies.

---

## 6. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
*   **Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR)**: Sum of all active hospital subscriptions.
*   **Lead Conversion Rate**: Percentage of patient inquiries that result in a confirmed treatment.
*   **Hospital Churn**: Rate at which hospitals downgrade or cancel subscriptions.
*   **Average Transaction Value (ATV)**: The average procedure cost processed through the platform.
